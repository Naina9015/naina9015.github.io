About

I am a computer science faculty member and researcher working on recommender systems. My PhD at IIT (BHU) Varanasi focused on recommendation models that are both accurate and diverse, so that users see more than the most popular or most obvious items. I teach undergraduate and postgraduate courses and enjoy connecting research questions with classroom teaching.

Research

  • Recommender systems
  • Diversity in recommendation
  • Graph neural networks
  • Heterogeneous information networks
  • Music information retrieval
  • NLP
  • Social networks

PhD thesis: Design and Development of Some Approaches for Accurate and Diverse Music Recommendation Systems (IIT (BHU) Varanasi, 2023).

Directions I am working on:

  • Open-set recommendation: recommending items from categories not seen in training data, so systems adapt as tastes change.
  • Contrastive recommendation for diversity: deliberately contrasting user preferences to widen the range of recommended content.
  • User-centric interfaces with IoT: using real-time context from smart devices, with privacy controls such as encryption and user-managed data sharing.
